LRC Reservation Policies

  • Reservations are subject to LRC policies and staff approval where required.
  • Maximum of 3 hours of booked study area time per student per day.
  • Students must observe the scheduled reservation period.
  • Cancellation requires at least 1 hour notice through the booking system.
  • Observe silence in individual study areas. No food or drinks inside the LRC.
  • Study areas must be used responsibly and left in order.
